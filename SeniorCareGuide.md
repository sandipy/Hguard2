# Senior Care & Halo Alert Guardian Guide
### 100% Hands-Free Always • Zero Setup • No Buttons to Press for Seniors

This system is specially engineered for elderly family members (including 90–95+ year-old seniors) living independently or needing discreet, continuous room and bathroom monitoring. 

**Core Principle:** The senior **NEVER has to press any button, touch the screen, or configure anything**. The phone is placed in their room or bathroom like a dedicated smart sentinel. It listens 24/7, detects falls optically from 5+ feet away, and auto-answers family calls out loud into the room.

## 3. How Daily Scenarios Work 100% Hands-Free

### Scenario A: Senior Calls for Help from Bed or Floor
- Senior shouts: **"Help!"** or **"Help me, I fell!"**
- Senior's phone responds out loud: *"Distress keyword detected. Alerting family now."*
- Family viewer sounds an alarm siren and speaks: *"Emergency! Senior called out for help at Bedroom!"*

### Scenario B: Senior Slips in the Bathroom (5+ ft away)
- The senior's phone senses the downward plunge to the floor and subsequent immobility.
- Camera announces: *"A fall was detected. Are you okay? Help is being notified."*
- Viewer receives urgent visual & audible siren: *"Emergency! Fall detected at Bathroom! Check senior immediately!"*

### Scenario C: False Alarm or Senior is Fine
- The senior does **not** have to crawl to the phone or push any button.
- Senior simply says: **"I am okay"** or **"False alarm"**.
- The system announces: *"Alert cleared. Family has been informed that you are okay."* The family viewer is immediately updated hands-free.

### Scenario D: Family Checks In via Walkie-Talkie
- Caregiver opens Viewer and taps **"Talk to Room"** or quick phrase: *"Mom, are you okay? Just checking in."*
- Senior's phone speaks the phrase into the room.
- Senior answers normally with their voice; their words are transcribed and streamed live to the caregiver.
