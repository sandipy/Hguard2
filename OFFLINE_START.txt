HGUARD OFFLINE SURVEILLANCE PACKAGE
===================================

1. Double click index.html in modern Chrome/Edge/Safari/Firefox.
2. Or run a simple local web server in this directory:
   python3 -m http.server 8080
   or
   npx serve .

3. Open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.

All features (3-Camera streams, Multi-view 1-screen monitor, Battery 80% guard, Eco-Cool blackout, Motion detection, Encrypted logs) run offline in your browser!
